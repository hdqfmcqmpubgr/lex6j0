  • Winds aloft: Open-Meteo pressure-level forecast, NOAA HRRR (3 km CONUS) (Open-Meteo does not report the model cycle), combined with NWS FB winds and temperatures aloft for CAE (aviationweather.gov; 6 h forecast used 10:00–17:00, 12 h forecast used 17:00–02:00, 24 h forecast used 02:00–14:00 EDT).   • Temperature/dewpoint and relative humidity: Surface: KCAE METAR now, NBM KCAE temperature/dewpoint for later hours; aloft: the same Open-Meteo model temperature and relative humidity. Carburetor icing potential calculated from temperature and dewpoint.
  • Cloud layers and mixing height: KCAE METAR for the current hour, KCAE TAF after that; mixing height from the NWS gridpoint forecast.
  • 6J0 Runway winds: 'Now' line: speed = average of the latest KCAE, KCUB, KAIK and KAGS METAR wind speeds; direction = distance-weighted vector average of the latest KCAE and KCUB METAR directions (skipping any that are calm, variable or over 45 min old); if neither qualifies, the KCAE/KCUB/KAIK/KAGS METAR vector average; if all are calm or variable, the latest RC Winds Lexington County Airport reading, shown ±22.5° (8-point compass); panel time = the oldest of those observations.   • Radar: NOAA NEXRAD Level II super-resolution reflectivity from the AWS open-data archive (KCAE, falling back to KCLX then KGSP): KCAE volume scan 11:00 EDT; lightning from GOES-19 GLM.
